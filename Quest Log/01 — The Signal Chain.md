# 01 — The Signal Chain

## 1. The First Confusion

You push a plastic stick with your hand. A plane on your screen banks left. Between those two moments, your physical movement became electricity, then numbers, then a message your computer understood. **What happened at each step?**

> [!tip]- Retrieval Cue
> A store-bought flight stick has a hand at one end and a game at the other. List every stage the "movement" passes through in between. What form does the information take at each stage?

> [!tip]- Retrieval Cue
> Why can't the computer just "feel" the stick moving? What does a computer actually understand as input?

## 2. Open One Up

> [!todo] Field Mission
> Find a teardown video or photo set of a cheap joystick or game controller (the Thrustmaster T.Flight is a great one). Identify: where the movement is measured, where the "brain" is, and how it connects to the PC. Screenshot or sketch it and drop it here.

> [!hint]- Hint
> Search "T.Flight HOTAS teardown" or "joystick teardown potentiometer".

## 3. Your Body Does This Too

> [!tip]- Retrieval Cue
> Your nervous system also turns physical movement into signals the brain understands. What is the body's version of each stage in the chain? Where does the analogy break down?

## 4. Feynman Check

> [!question]- Feynman Check
> Explain to a friend who's never touched electronics how pushing a stick makes a plane on screen turn, in under a minute and with no jargon you can't define.
