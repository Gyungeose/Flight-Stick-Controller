# 03 — Sensing Motion

## 1. Measuring an Angle

The stick tilts. Something has to turn "how far it tilted" into electricity.

> [!tip]- Retrieval Cue
> What is a potentiometer? What actually changes inside it when you turn the knob, and how does that become a voltage?

> [!tip]- Retrieval Cue
> What is a voltage divider, and why is it the key to understanding a potentiometer?

## 2. The Magnet Way

> [!tip]- Retrieval Cue
> What is the Hall effect? Who discovered it, and how can a magnet and a chip measure an angle without anything touching?

> [!tip]- Retrieval Cue
> Why do flight-sim enthusiasts complain about potentiometers after a year or two of use? What physically happens to them?

> [!todo] Field Mission
> Find a review or forum thread comparing a pot-based stick to a Hall-based one. Note the specific complaints and praises. What words do users use for the problems: "spiking"? "drift"? "deadzone"? Define each one.

## 3. From Voltage to Number

> [!tip]- Retrieval Cue
> The board reads a voltage but your PC needs a number. What is an ADC (analog-to-digital converter)? What does "10-bit resolution" mean, and how many distinct positions can a 10-bit ADC tell apart?

> [!tip]- Retrieval Cue
> You've met sampling and resolution before, in a heart. Where? How do those ideas carry over here?

## 4. Feynman Check

> [!question]- Feynman Check
> Explain pots vs Hall sensors to someone choosing between two joysticks in a store. Which would you start with for *your* build, which would you finish with, and why?
