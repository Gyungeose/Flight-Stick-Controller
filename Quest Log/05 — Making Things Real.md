# 05 — Making Things Real

## 1. Two Ways to Print

> [!tip]- Retrieval Cue
> How does an FDM (filament) printer build a part? How does an SLA (resin) printer do it? Draw both processes.

> [!tip]- Retrieval Cue
> Why would a medical illustration centre likely own one type over the other? What does each type do *best*?

> [!todo] Field Mission
> Ask SGU's medical illustration centre what printer they have. Before you go, write down every question you need answered, and *why* each answer matters to your design.

## 2. Materials Under Stress

> [!tip]- Retrieval Cue
> What's the difference between a material being **strong**, **stiff**, **tough**, and **brittle**? (These are not the same thing.)

> [!tip]- Retrieval Cue
> A gimbal has springs pulling on it constantly and gets flexed thousands of times. What is **fatigue**? Which printing materials handle it well, and which crack?

> [!tip]- Retrieval Cue
> Grenada is hot. What happens to common printing plastics in heat, and which one would you not leave in a car?

## 3. The Grain of a Print

> [!tip]- Retrieval Cue
> Why is a printed part weaker in one direction than another? How does that change *how you place a part on the print bed*?

> [!tip]- Retrieval Cue
> Why do printed holes come out smaller than you drew them? What's a "tolerance test" and how would you design one?

> [!hint]- Hint
> Search "3D printing design for FDM tolerances" and "print orientation strength".
